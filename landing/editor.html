First time you open it: the build is ad-hoc unsigned for now (notarized signing coming soon). On recent macOS, opening the .app from the .dmg may pop "Parallax Editor is damaged and can't be opened" — that's Gatekeeper, not the app. Run this once in Terminal to clear the quarantine flag: 

xattr -cr "/Applications/Parallax Editor.app"

Then double-click as normal. (If macOS still asks "are you sure you want to open this", right-click the app once → Open.) Compatible with Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.
